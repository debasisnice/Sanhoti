Sanhoti — Bengali Association of Orange County, California

Sanhoti (সংহতি) is a non-profit 501(c)(3) Bengali cultural association serving Orange County and Southern California. We celebrate Durga Puja, Saraswati Puja, Poila Boishakh (Bengali New Year), and host Bengali concerts, cultural programs, and community events for families in Costa Mesa, Irvine, Tustin, Rancho Santa Margarita, Mission Viejo, and across SoCal.
